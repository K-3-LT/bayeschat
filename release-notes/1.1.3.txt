Release date: October 14, 2026


English (What's New)
--------------------
• Custom providers — OpenCode gateways (Go and Zen) now work end to end: each model is routed to the protocol it expects, and the per-conversation session header is handled for you
• Gemini fixed — Gemini providers could neither chat nor list models because they pointed at a path that does not exist; they now use Gemini's OpenAI-compatible surface, and providers you already saved correct themselves
• Provider edits apply immediately — changing a provider's URL, key, or headers now takes effect on the next send, regenerate, edit, or title instead of waiting until you reselect the model

Also in this update:
• Custom Headers support the {session_id} and {model_id} placeholders, with OpenCode and LiteLLM examples, and a crash when removing a header row is fixed
• Jump-to-bottom stays available while generating, and no longer appears for short replies that already fit on screen
• Pinned messages hold still while reasoning or tool cards expand and while a reply streams
• Fixed a card collapse tap that could read as dead while reasoning was painting
• OpenCode presets are gone from the provider picker; those gateways are still added by hand with their base URL
